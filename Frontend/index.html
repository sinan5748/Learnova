About AI PDF Assistant

AI PDF Assistant is an intelligent study platform designed to help students learn from their PDF documents easily. It uses Artificial Intelligence to make studying, understanding and exam preparation simpler.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to save students' time and make learning more effective by transforming ordinary PDF documents into interactive study materials.

Key Features

  • Chat with PDF documents
  • Generate PDF summaries
  • Create exam answers for 2, 5 and 10 marks
  • Generate interactive MCQ quizzes
  • Store and manage uploaded PDF documents

Technologies Used
